## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS, static export, hosted on Cloudflare Pages. No database, CMS or own backend; external services for payments (Stripe Payment Links, Cal.com), forms (Web3Forms) and newsletter (MailerLite). Details in CLAUDE.md.

## Users

- **Primary:** young adults in Bucharest (roughly 20–40) who discover AVA on Instagram, usually on their phone. They want a calm, creative evening, to meet people, or to get to know themselves better, and most have never touched clay.
- Secondary (confirmed by the service list, not ranked by the user): HR and team leads booking teambuilding or wellbeing workshops; people looking for 1:1 personal-development counselling; parents booking private workshops for children; schools ("Școala altfel").

## Product Purpose

AVA Art & Soul is Alessia Văideanu's practice: clay workshops, counselling and retreats where working with clay is the way into self-knowledge and connection ("autocunoaștere prin creație").

The homepage succeeds when a first-time visitor **remembers the brand and its idea**. The best case is that they **book a place at a workshop**.

## Positioning

Workshops facilitated by someone who is both a ceramic artist and an accredited personal-development counsellor. The clay is the tool, and the conversation around the table is the point. The workshops are small (max. 8 people) and themed, with home-made tea and snacks, and the piece is fired and kept.

## Operating Context

- Monthly themed group workshops in Bucharest (Cotroceni and partner locations), 3 hours, from 185 lei.
- Companies: on-site or at partner venues, 4–16 people.
- Counselling: 60–90 min, online or in person.
- Private 1:1 workshops for adults and children.
- Retreats ("coming soon").
- Pieces are collected after drying and firing (4–6 weeks).
- Places are confirmed after full payment; the cancellation rule is 24 hours' notice.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name and lockup:** "AVA Art & Soul", tagline "autocunoaștere prin creație". The logo is the clay vase, "AVA" and "ART & SOUL".
- **Brand identity PDFs:** palette Lut ars, Nisip, Nisip deschis, Teracotă, Argilă, Salvie; graphic element "inelele de lut"; ceramic stamp.
- **Fonts:** the PDFs specify Fraunces and Jost, but the user asked to explore less generic fonts for the site (decision open). Pinyon Script is requested as the accent.
- **Voice:** first person (Alessia), warm, simple, never corporate.
- **Requested feel:** elegant, calm, rich; diffuse light from beige to light brown; small "woodsprite" lights that follow the visitor, with clean, smooth animation.
- **Must not feel:** corporate or slick, or dark and heavy.

## Product Principles

1. The brand idea has to land in one viewport: clay as a way back to yourself, with other people around the same table.
2. Warmth over polish. It should feel like being invited to the table, not sold to.
3. Truthful by default: real photos, real dates, no invented proof, and careful legal wording.
4. Phone first, because the visitor arrives from an Instagram link.
5. Calm motion that rewards attention and never demands it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Romanian diacritics must render correctly (ș, ț with a comma below). Motion must respect reduced-motion settings. Text contrast is AA. Tap targets are at least 44 px.
